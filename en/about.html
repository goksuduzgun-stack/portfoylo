My story

Code, ink, and music.

I'm studying Computer Programming at Akdeniz University. I'm building my skills in programming fundamentals, web interfaces, and databases by practising what I learn. I believe the best way to understand something is to try it and make something with it.

I'm also a tattoo artist, turning my own drawings into tattoos. The process has taught me about line, proportion, and patience. In my spare time, I play electric guitar and let music fuel my creativity.

In my software work, I care about user experience—UX—and making interfaces clear and accessible.
