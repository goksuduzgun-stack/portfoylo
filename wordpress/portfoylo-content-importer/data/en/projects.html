My independent venture · Owner and sole employee

Vybee: an end-to-end product for the beauty industry.

Visit Vybee ↗

Vybee is a B2B SaaS and mobile product I am building to connect hair and beauty salons with their customers through real-time appointments and business operations.

I own Vybee and am its sole employee. I personally carry the work from product direction through software development, building the iOS and Android apps with Flutter from a shared codebase.

01

Salon discovery & booking

Location-based discovery helps customers find nearby businesses, while the real-time booking flow focuses on making appointment availability easy to follow.

02

Live calendar sync

Live calendar synchronisation is designed to keep customer appointments and salon operations up to date.

03

In-app communication

Instant messaging between customers and hairdressers brings appointment-related communication into the app.

04

Vybee Pro analytics

Business-specific revenue and appointment analytics help salons follow their operations and performance.

Technical foundation

I approach the product's technical foundation as a connected whole, from mobile clients and data security to error monitoring.
